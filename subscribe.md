New poems and essays are published through a feed, which a feed reader app checks for you and delivers as they appear. There is no email list and nothing to sign up for.

To follow along, copy this address into your feed reader:

<p class="feed-address"><a href="{{ '/feed.xml' | absolute_url }}">{{ '/feed.xml' | absolute_url }}</a></p>

If you don't have a feed reader yet, free ones include Feedly and Inoreader on any device, and NetNewsWire on Apple devices. Most readers will also find the feed if you simply paste in the address of this site.
