*On the Fated Births of Brothers Æþelwulf and Bodewulf*

Hid-twinkling Twins yonder.  
Mother of Wolves rests her  
head as the bright moon,  
full and flush, sits over toon.  
Aethelings and good bodings,  
not-twins, yet twinnings.
