*(or how I came to understand the vegans’ cause)*

Boys be good for fattened food:  
No breasts that swell in them, nor uterine physique.  
Robbed from breast at behest of those in mood  
for fattened food.

This thing is rare, or medium.  
That thing is quite unique.  
Dainty fare for triclinium;  
(Younglings, only, are fit for ‘em.)

Out there, beyond, they’d long suckle.  
(We are sitting, sucking on perique.)  
In here, within, we’d think them cuckolds.  
(Or, at least, mere ramping beasts in muckle.)

Anyways, taking that gilded cream.  
(At least it once was caressed by fingers’ technique.)  
Technique now set to manage screams  
of putrid lives in squalor’s steam.

Knashing teeth of sudden grief,  
muching corn, not blade, quite bleak.  
For fatness all ‘round, and unto kine-folk-fiefs:  
Fatened, fatness, fatening thieves!

Behold, I am Gilbert, at the plate.  
Imago Dei, or steward? Speak!  
These cheeses here for me to sate,  
By fleshly considerations ate.

Behold, Mother Earth, from warring members;  
“A law unto… groaning” approach the peak!  
Spears, like stuck pigs, stabbed into your babies’ members;  
Viscera and bones rot to smouldering embers.

But, oh, is coming fire gleaming  
and wings of fiery bird consuming  
a recreated set of forms by such as did at first into them speak:  
renewing!
