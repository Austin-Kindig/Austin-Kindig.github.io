title: "Glutting"
categories: poems
tags: [pumpkins, harvest, autumn, gluttony, appetite, hearth]
---

I scratch, screech, and squeak out the sheet  
from the craggy coals that crackle beneath.

High above the iron valley, pumpkin pillows  
sit like hillocks, swollen and yellow,  
as frothing fog filters up into light, ghastly tendrils  
groping moon-beams bright till the sheen’d skins wrinkle.

Turning them over, I see the succulent sinews,  
and with hot hands seething  
moist flesh I scoop and scrape to carve voided hollows-  
caverns to slather where oozing cream wallows.

Carroty scalps go to the scrap  
for chickens to scratch.

Salt, pepper, and forky furrows,  
and the gulping gullet swallows  
until those yielding gilded fruits  
are past butter’d lips.

There are not enough pumpkins.
